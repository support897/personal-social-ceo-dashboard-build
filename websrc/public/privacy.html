Ruefly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Ruefly is a social content planner for creators. Each person who signs up gets their own private account. It is operated by Ilse Placencia (hello@ruefly.xyz).

Signing in

You sign in with Google, with Apple, or with your email and a password you choose. Accounts are run by Firebase Authentication (a Google service). Your password is held by Firebase in hashed form; we never see it or store it ourselves. If you use the demo instead, the app creates a temporary anonymous account on Firebase, holding only the made-up sample data. Deleting the demo deletes that account and its data.

What the app holds

  • Your name, the one you give at sign-up (or the name on your Google or Apple account).
  • Your email address, used to sign you in and for password resets.
  • Your account ID, a code Firebase gives your account. Every record in the app is kept under it.
  • The content you put in: your plans, schedules, drafts and the posts you track.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising. No ad networks.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs. Nothing is tracked across apps or websites.
  • This version plans, schedules inside the app, reminds and tracks. It does not post to any social network, and it does not hold your social network passwords.
  • Your data is never sold. It is not shared with third parties, except the services named on this page that run the app.

AI writing help

When you use the AI writing help, your prompt and the caption text you are working on are sent to the AI provider you pick, under your own account and your own key. Your key stays on your device and Ruefly does not keep it. The key is removed from the device when you sign out or delete your account. What comes back is saved only as your drafts. It is never posted.

Notifications

Reminders are made on your device itself, in the phone app and in the web version alike. Nothing is sent to a notification service for them.

Where it lives

Your account and content are stored in Firebase (Google Cloud) in Sydney, Australia, and travel over encrypted connections. The database rules keep every account separate: only your signed-in account can read your data.

Your choices

  • Delete your account and all its content inside the app: open Account and choose Delete my account. It happens straight away.
  • You can also ask by email: hello@ruefly.xyz. Email deletions are completed within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new date at the top.
